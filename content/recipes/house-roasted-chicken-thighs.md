## Ingredients

- 800 g chicken thighs (about 6), bone in and skin on
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a tray with foil.
2. Pat the skin dry with kitchen paper. Wet skin steams and stays pale; this is the only step that decides whether it crisps.
3. Put the thighs on the tray skin-side up, add the oil, and scatter the salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion powder over them.
4. Rub it all in with your hands, getting under and over each thigh, then turn them skin-side up again and leave a gap between them.
5. Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, until the skin is deep golden and crackling and the juices run clear when a thigh is pierced at the bone.

## Notes

Granules rather than fresh garlic. Fresh burns in half an hour at this heat and turns bitter; granules brown and stay sweet.

There is room on the tray, and something should be in it. Broccoli or baby corn tossed in oil and salt roasts in the same time — see the roasted broccoli and baby corn recipe for how far to take it.

## Storage

Keeps three days in the fridge. The skin will not crisp again in a microwave.

Reheat at 200 °C for 8 minutes, or in a dry pan skin-side down for 4 minutes.

Strip any meat left on the bones and keep the bones — they make the stock for the chicken and sweetcorn soup.
