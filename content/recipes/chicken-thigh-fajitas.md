## Ingredients

- 640 g chicken thighs (about 5), boneless and skinless
- 2 bell peppers, sliced into 2 cm strips
- 1 onion, sliced into 2 cm wedges
- 1.5 tbsp olive oil
- 1.5 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper
- 1 lime, halved

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a large tray with foil.
2. Stir the paprika, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, chilli powder, salt and pepper together in a small bowl until the colour is even.
3. Put the chicken, peppers and onion straight onto the tray. Add the oil, then the spice mix.
4. Rub everything in with your hands for 60 seconds, until each piece is uniformly red-brown and no dry spice is left on the foil. Spread it into a single layer.
5. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until the chicken juices run clear and the pepper edges have blackened in places.
6. For more char, put it under the grill for the last 2 to 3 minutes and watch it — spice rubs go from charred to burnt in under a minute.
7. Squeeze the lime over the hot tray the moment it comes out, while it can still sizzle.

## Notes

Thick strips, not thin. Thin peppers collapse to nothing in half an hour at this heat.

The lime goes on hot and out of the oven, never before. Squeezed on beforehand it steams the tray; squeezed on after, it lifts the whole thing.

## Storage

Keeps three days in the fridge and is the better for it — the spices settle into the meat overnight.

Reheat in a dry frying pan over high heat for 3 minutes to get some of the char back. The microwave makes the peppers watery.

Freezes for two months.
