## Ingredients

- 1 kg chicken thighs (about 8), boneless and skinless
- 3 tbsp ginger, minced
- 2 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 200 g jasmine rice (1 cup)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 4 spring onions, white parts only

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Lay the chicken in the middle of a large sheet of heavy-duty foil. Rub in 2 tbsp of the ginger, the shaoxing wine and 1 tsp of the salt, working it into every surface.
3. Bring the long edges of the foil together above the chicken and fold them over three times, then fold each end over twice. The parcel must be airtight — anything that escapes as steam is liquid that will not be on the plate.
4. Bake on a tray for 30 to 35 minutes.
5. While it bakes, rinse the rice under cold water, working it with your fingers, until the water runs clear rather than milky. Put it in a rice cooker and fill to the 1-cup line.
6. Stir the oil, the remaining 1 tbsp ginger, the garlic and the remaining 0.5 tsp salt into the water. Lay the spring onion whites on top and start the cooker.
7. When it switches to keep-warm, leave it 10 minutes without opening it. The grains at the top are still firm before this and give in that time.
8. Open the parcel away from you — the first escape of steam is scalding. Serve the chicken over the rice and pour every drop of the juice in the foil over the top.

## Notes

The juice in the parcel is the point of the dish. Cook it uncovered and you get the same chicken and none of the sauce.

Boneless thighs only. A breast dries out in a sealed parcel at this temperature, and bones stop the meat lying flat enough to seal.

## Storage

Keeps three days in the fridge, chicken and rice stored separately — rice left sitting in the juice goes soft.

Reheat covered, in a pan over low heat, with 2 tbsp of water for 6 minutes. The microwave works for the rice and toughens the chicken.

Do not leave cooked rice at room temperature for more than an hour. Cool it fast, refrigerate it, and reheat it once only.
