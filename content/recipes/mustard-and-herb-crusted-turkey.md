## Ingredients

- 400 g turkey breast (2 breasts)
- 2 tbsp dijon mustard
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 0.75 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper
- 15 g walnuts (a small handful), crushed

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a tray with foil.
2. Lay each breast between two sheets of baking paper and beat it with a rolling pin until it is an even 2 cm thick throughout. A tapered breast cooks to done at the thin end and stays raw at the thick one.
3. Brush the mustard over the top and sides in a layer thick enough that you cannot see the meat through it.
4. Scatter the thyme, salt, pepper and crushed walnuts over the mustard and press them down with the back of a spoon until they stay put.
5. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes. It is done when the crust is dry to the touch and the juices run clear from the thickest part.
6. Rest 5 minutes before slicing, or the juice ends up on the board.

## Notes

Turkey breast has almost no fat and goes from done to dry in about two minutes. Take it out as soon as the juices run clear rather than waiting for colour.

Wholegrain mustard works and gives a rougher crust. Dijon holds the herbs better.

## Storage

Keeps three days in the fridge and is better cold in a sandwich than reheated — reheating dries it further.

If reheating, cover and use 160 °C for 8 minutes rather than a microwave.

Freezes for two months, sliced, with baking paper between the slices.
