## Ingredients

- 640 g chicken thighs (about 5), boneless and skinless
- 180 g greek yogurt (a small pot)
- 1.5 tsp garlic powder
- 1.5 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1.25 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 15 g parmesan
- 400 g broccoli (1 large head), cut into bite-sized florets
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 0.5 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a large tray with foil.
2. Trim the loose fat from the thighs and unroll them so each lies flat. Pat both faces dry with kitchen paper — yoghurt slides off a wet surface instead of setting on it.
3. Stir the yoghurt, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, 0.75 tsp of the salt, 0.75 tsp of the pepper and the parmesan together into a thick paste.
4. Lay the chicken on one half of the tray and spoon the paste over each piece, covering the top completely and about 3 mm deep.
5. Put the broccoli on the other half. Add the oil, the remaining 0.5 tsp salt, the remaining 0.25 tsp pepper and the chilli flakes, and toss until coated. Spread it into a single layer with the florets not touching.
6. Bake the whole tray for 25 to 30 minutes. It is done when the yoghurt crust has set and browned in patches and the juices run clear when a thigh is pierced at its thickest point, and when the broccoli tips are charred.
7. If the broccoli is still pale, lift the chicken onto a plate to rest and give the tray another 5 minutes.
8. Rest the chicken 5 minutes. Squeeze the lemon over the broccoli just before serving.

## Notes

Use 0% yoghurt. Full-fat splits under the heat and runs off the meat into the tray.

The crust does not brown like cheese does; it sets, dries and takes brown patches. Waiting for an even golden top will overcook the chicken.

## Storage

Keeps three days in the fridge. The crust softens overnight and does not come back crisp.

Reheat covered at 180 °C for 10 minutes. The broccoli is better eaten cold in a salad than reheated.
