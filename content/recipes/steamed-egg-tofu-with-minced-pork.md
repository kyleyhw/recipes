## Ingredients

- 150 g fatty pork mince
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp cornflour
- 0.5 tsp granulated sugar
- 0.25 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 10 g ginger, finely chopped
- 250 g egg tofu (1 tube)
- 15 g spring onions, finely sliced

## Method

1. Put the pork in a bowl with the soy sauce, shaoxing wine, cornflour, sugar, white pepper, sesame oil and ginger. Stir hard in one direction for 45 seconds, until it clings together in one mass on the spoon. Stop there — this is not a steamed patty and it does not want the long stir those need.
2. Slit the tofu tube down its length and slide the tofu out. Cut it into rounds 2 cm thick and lay them flat on a heatproof plate with a rim, slightly apart rather than overlapping.
3. Take teaspoon-sized pieces of the pork and press one onto each round, flattening it to about 5 mm so it covers the face. Any left over goes in the gaps between them.
4. Put a rack in a wok or wide pan, add water to just below it, and bring it to a boil, then drop to a steady simmer.
5. Set the plate on the rack, cover, and steam for 12 minutes. It is done when the pork has firmed and turned from pink to pale grey-brown all through, a knife into the thickest piece comes out clean, and a clear broth has gathered on the plate.
6. Scatter the spring onion over and serve on the plate it steamed on, with rice. The broth goes over the rice.

## Notes

Thin pork, not lumps. Five millimetres over the face of each round cooks in the
same twelve minutes the tofu wants; a thumb-sized ball does not, and by the time
its middle is done the tofu underneath has gone to sponge.

Forty-five seconds of stirring, not three minutes. Steamed Pork Patty with
Preserved Bean Curd and its four siblings are stirred long and hard to build a
protein gel that holds a whole patty together. Here the tofu is doing that job
and the pork only has to hold onto itself, so the same technique taken that far
would make it springy for no reason.

Compare it with Braised Egg Tofu with Minced Pork, which is the same three
ingredients and a different dish: that one fries the tofu and thickens a gravy,
this one steams everything at once and produces a clear broth instead. This is
the weeknight one — one plate, no oil, nothing to wash but the plate.

Fatty mince at 20% if you can get it. The fat renders into the broth and is most
of what makes it taste of anything; 5% mince steams into grey grit.

A steady simmer rather than a rolling boil, as with everything made from this
tube. The pork does not mind, but the tofu does.

## Storage

Three days in the fridge, on the plate, covered, in its broth. It reheats
completely — steam for 5 minutes or microwave covered for 2, and there is
nothing about it that suffers.

The assembled raw plate can be made in the morning and steamed in the evening;
keep it covered in the fridge and give it 14 minutes rather than 12.

It does not freeze.
