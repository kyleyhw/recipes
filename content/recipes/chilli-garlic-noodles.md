## Ingredients

- 200 g dried wheat noodles
- 6 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp chilli flakes
- 2 tsp granulated sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp black vinegar

## Method

1. Bring 2 litres of water to a rolling boil. Cook the noodles for the time on the packet, then drain and divide between two bowls.
2. While they cook, pile the garlic, chilli flakes, sugar and salt on top of the noodles in a heap. Do not stir.
3. Heat the oil in a small pan over high heat for 2 minutes, until a chilli flake dropped in sizzles hard and rises immediately.
4. Pour the oil directly over the heap. It will hiss and foam for 5 seconds — that is the garlic cooking, and it is the whole dish.
5. Add the soy sauce and vinegar, then toss for 30 seconds until every strand is coated and glossy.
6. Taste. It should be sharp enough to make you blink; add vinegar a teaspoon at a time until it does.

## Notes

The oil must be hot enough to cook the garlic on contact. Below that it steams instead, and the dish tastes raw and harsh.

Three tablespoons of chilli flakes is not a mistake.

## Storage

Best eaten immediately — the noodles soften and the garlic loses its edge within the hour.

The chilli oil itself keeps three weeks in a sealed jar at room temperature. Make it in quantity and pour it over noodles as needed; that is the version worth planning for.
