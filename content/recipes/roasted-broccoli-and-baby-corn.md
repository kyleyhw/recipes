## Ingredients

- 400 g broccoli (1 large head), cut into bite-sized florets
- 200 g baby corn (about 25 ears)
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a large tray with foil.
2. Put the broccoli and baby corn on the tray, drizzle over the oil, and add the salt and pepper.
3. Toss with your hands until every floret is coated and no dry patches are left — dry florets steam rather than roast.
4. Spread everything into a single layer with a gap between pieces. Use two trays rather than crowd one: the vegetables give off water, and a crowded tray traps it.
5. Roast for 15 minutes, until the broccoli tips are dark brown to black and crisp enough to snap, and the corn is blistered in patches.

## Notes

The black tips are the dish, not a mistake. Pull it out when it looks slightly too far gone and it will be right.

Cut the florets so they all have one flat face and lay them cut-side down. That face is the one that browns.

## Storage

Keeps three days in the fridge, but it will not be crisp again.

Reheat in a hot oven or an air fryer at 200 °C for 5 minutes; a microwave turns it limp and sulphurous. Cold, chopped, it is better in a salad than reheated.
