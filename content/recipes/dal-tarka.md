## Ingredients

- 250 g red lentils
- 900 ml water
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 2 dried red chillies, broken in half
- 1 tsp fine salt
- 1 lemon

## Method

1. Rinse the lentils in cold water until the water runs clear, about four changes of water.
2. Put the lentils, water and turmeric in a saucepan. Bring to a boil over high heat, then lower to a bare simmer.
3. Simmer uncovered for 25 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes, until the lentils collapse when pressed against the side of the pan and the mixture drops thickly from a spoon.
4. Stir in the salt and take the pan off the heat.
5. Heat the ghee in a small frying pan over medium-high heat until it shimmers, about 1 minute.
6. Add the cumin seeds. They will sizzle and darken within 15 seconds — as soon as they smell toasted, add the garlic and chillies.
7. Fry for 45 seconds, until the garlic is golden at the edges but not brown in the middle. It burns in seconds past this point.
8. Pour the contents of the frying pan over the lentils, scraping in every bit of the ghee. Stir once, leaving streaks.
9. Squeeze over half the lemon and taste. Add more salt and lemon until it tastes sharp rather than flat.

## Notes

Keeps three days in the fridge and thickens as it sits; loosen with hot water when reheating.

The tarka is the dish. Bloom the spices properly and it is worth cooking; rush them and it is lentil soup.
