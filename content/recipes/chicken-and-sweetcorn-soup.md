## Ingredients

- 325 g sweetcorn (1 tin)
- 500 ml chicken stock
- 200 g chicken breast (1 breast), cooked and shredded
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp water
- 1 egg, beaten
- 2 spring onions, chopped

## Method

1. Pour the stock and sweetcorn into a saucepan, bring to a boil, then drop to a simmer.
2. Stir in the chicken, soy sauce and sesame oil and simmer 2 minutes.
3. Stir the cornflour into the cold water until no lumps remain — cold, always; cornflour meeting hot liquid sets into strings before it can disperse.
4. Pour the slurry into the simmering soup, stirring, and keep stirring for 1 to 2 minutes until it turns glossy and thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
5. Turn the heat off. Stir the soup in a slow circle with one hand and pour the beaten egg in a thin thread with the other. It sets on contact into ribbons; stop stirring the moment the last of it is in, or the ribbons break into specks.
6. Serve topped with the spring onions.

## Notes

Creamed corn gives a thicker soup and needs less slurry. Drained kernels give a clearer one. Both are correct; do not use both at once.

The soup must not be boiling when the egg goes in. Boiling scrambles it.

## Storage

Keeps two days in the fridge. The cornflour thins on standing and the egg goes rubbery, so it is worth eating the same day.

Reheat gently, without boiling, and stir. Do not freeze — a cornflour-thickened soup separates on thawing and will not come back.
